Titles

Tick the titles to rip. Episode numbers renumber themselves; "Special" puts a title in Season 0. 4K UHD drives can stop reading a disc that sits idle for a few minutes (LibreDrive "sleep bug") - if a rip fails after a long review, eject and re-insert the disc.

Discs Needing Review

Automatic Mode parks a disc here instead of guessing when the detectors disagree or flag a title it would rip (too long, unusual length, conflicting episode names). Titles that failed to rip also land here for a retry. Put the disc in a drive before clicking "Rip These".

Shows

Each show remembers its season and the next episode number, so you can switch between series (or rip two at once in two drives). "Next episode" moves on by itself after every disc.
